## Global Oil Markets on High Alert
The recent denial by Iran of any agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz has sent shockwaves through the global oil markets. This strategic waterway, which connects the Persian Gulf to the Arabian Sea, is a critical passage for international oil trade, with approximately 20% of the world's oil supply passing through it. Iran's stance on the Strait has significant implications for the global economy, and investors are closely watching the situation unfold.

### Historical Context
The Strait of Hormuz has long been a point of contention between Iran and the international community. In the past, Iran has threatened to close the Strait in response to economic sanctions or military action. The most recent incident occurred in 2019, when Iran seized a British-flagged oil tanker, further escalating tensions in the region. The current situation is particularly sensitive, given the ongoing conflict in the Middle East and the delicate balance of power in the region.
