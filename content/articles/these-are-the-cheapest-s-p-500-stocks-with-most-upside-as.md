### Market Impact: The Role of Valuations
Valuations play a crucial role in determining the potential upside of a stock. Stocks that are undervalued, as measured by their price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio, often present attractive buying opportunities for investors. The P/E ratio, which compares a company's stock price to its earnings per share, serves as a key metric for evaluating a stock's valuation. A lower P/E ratio indicates that a stock is undervalued, while a higher ratio suggests overvaluation.
