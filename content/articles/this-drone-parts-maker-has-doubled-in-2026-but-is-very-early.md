### Risk Factors and Challenges
While Unusual Machines' growth prospects appear promising, there are potential risks and challenges that investors should be aware of. Additionally, the company faces intense competition from established players and new entrants, which could lead to pricing pressure and margin compression. Furthermore, Unusual Machines' reliance on a limited number of customers and suppliers poses a concentration risk, which could have a significant impact on the company's financial performance if any of these relationships are disrupted.
