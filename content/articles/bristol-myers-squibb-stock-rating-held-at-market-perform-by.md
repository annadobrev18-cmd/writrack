## Bristol-Myers Squibb: An Overview
Bristol-Myers Squibb is a multinational pharmaceutical company that has been a major player in the healthcare industry for over a century. The company was formed in 1989 through the merger of Bristol-Myers and Squibb Corporation. Headquartered in New York City, Bristol-Myers Squibb is known for its diverse portfolio of pharmaceutical products, including prescription medications, biologics, and vaccines.

### Business Segments
Bristol-Myers Squibb operates through two main business segments: Pharmaceuticals and Virology. The Pharmaceuticals segment includes products such as Orencia, Yervoy, and Empliciti, which are used to treat various types of cancer, autoimmune diseases, and other conditions. The Virology segment includes products such as Reyataz and Sustiva, which are used to treat HIV and other viral infections.
