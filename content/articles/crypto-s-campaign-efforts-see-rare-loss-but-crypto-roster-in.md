### Technical Analysis: Crypto Regulations and the Role of Congress

The regulatory landscape for crypto is complex and involves multiple stakeholders, including Congress, regulatory agencies, and industry bodies. The role of Congress is crucial in shaping the regulatory environment, as lawmakers have the power to introduce and pass legislation that affects the crypto industry.

#### Key Regulatory Bodies and Their Roles

* **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)**: The SEC is responsible for regulating securities and has been actively involved in crypto enforcement actions.
* **Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)**: The CFTC regulates commodities and has jurisdiction over crypto derivatives.
* **Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN)**: FinCEN is responsible for regulating anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) requirements for crypto businesses.
