### Historical Context of Hormuz Tensions

To understand the current situation, it's essential to delve into the historical context of Hormuz tensions. The Strait of Hormuz has long been a point of contention, with the US and its allies seeking to maintain freedom of navigation and Iran attempting to exert its influence over the region. The current tensions can be traced back to the US withdrawal from the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA) in 2018, which led to a significant escalation in tensions between the two nations.

#### Key Events Leading to Current Tensions

Several key events have contributed to the current tensions in the Strait of Hormuz:
* The US withdrawal from the JCPOA in 2018
* The reimposition of sanctions on Iran by the US
* The downing of a US drone by Iran in June 2019
* The seizure of a British-flagged oil tanker by Iran in July 2019
