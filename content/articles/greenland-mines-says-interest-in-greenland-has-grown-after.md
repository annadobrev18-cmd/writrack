### Historical Context
Greenland has been a topic of interest for various countries, including the United States, due to its strategic location and abundant natural resources. In 1946, the US offered to buy Greenland from Denmark for $100 million, but the offer was declined. Since then, there have been occasional discussions about the potential purchase of the island, but none have come to fruition.

Greenland is believed to have significant deposits of rare earth elements, including neodymium, dysprosium, and cerium.
