### Biohaven's Pipeline: A Review
Biohaven's pipeline is focused on the development of innovative treatments for neurological and rare diseases. The company's lead asset, rimegepant, is a orally administered calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) receptor antagonist for the treatment of migraine. Biohaven has also initiated clinical trials for other pipeline assets, including troriluzole, a novel glutamate modulator for the treatment of spinocerebellar ataxia and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS).
