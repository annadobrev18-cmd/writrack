The recent surge in Robinhood Chain's real-world assets, which has seen a fivefold increase, is a testament to the growing adoption of tokenized stocks. This phenomenon has been gaining traction in the cryptocurrency and blockchain space, with more investors seeking to capitalize on the benefits of tokenization.

### What are Tokenized Stocks?
Tokenized stocks refer to the digital representation of traditional securities, such as shares of publicly traded companies, on a blockchain network. This allows for the creation of a digital asset that can be traded, stored, and transferred in a similar manner to cryptocurrencies. The benefits of tokenized stocks include increased liquidity, reduced transaction costs, and improved accessibility for investors.
