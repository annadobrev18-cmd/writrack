### Historical Context: SpaceX's Rise to Prominence
Founded in 2002 by Elon Musk, SpaceX has come a long way since its inception. The company's early success was marked by the launch of its first rocket, the Falcon 1, in 2008. However, it was the successful launch of the Falcon 9 rocket in 2010 that really put SpaceX on the map. Since then, the company has gone on to achieve numerous milestones, including the first commercial spacecraft to dock with the International Space Station and the successful landing of reusable rockets.
