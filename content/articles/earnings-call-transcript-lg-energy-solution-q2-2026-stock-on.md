### Q2 2026 Earnings Call Highlights
The Q2 2026 earnings call transcript reveals that LGES reported a net income of $235 million, which is a significant decline from the $315 million reported in Q2 2025. The company's EPS came in at $0.85, missing the consensus estimate of $1.05. The revenue for the quarter stood at $2.35 billion, a 10% decrease from the same period last year.

#### Key Factors Contributing to the EPS Miss
Several factors contributed to the EPS miss, including:

* **Increased competition**: The battery market has become increasingly competitive, with new entrants and established players expanding their production capacity. This has led to downward pressure on prices, affecting LGES's profit margins.
* **Higher raw material costs**: The prices of key raw materials, such as lithium and cobalt, have increased significantly, leading to higher production costs for LGES.
* **Investment in new technologies**: LGES has been investing heavily in the development of new battery technologies, including solid-state batteries and lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) batteries. While these investments are expected to pay off in the long term, they have resulted in higher R&D expenses in the short term.
