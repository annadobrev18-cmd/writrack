## Fundamentals of Sotera Health
Sotera Health (SHC) is a leading global provider of mission-critical end-to-end sterilization solutions and lab testing and advisory services for the healthcare industry. The company's services are designed to protect the health and well-being of patients by providing a safe and effective way to sterilize medical instruments and equipment.

### Business Model
Sotera Health's business model is built around its sterilization services, which include gamma radiation, ethylene oxide, and electron beam sterilization. The company also provides lab testing and advisory services to help its customers ensure the quality and safety of their products. This comprehensive approach has enabled Sotera Health to establish long-term relationships with its customers, who rely on the company's expertise and capabilities to maintain the highest standards of quality and safety.

A DCF analysis is a widely used method for valuing companies. This approach involves estimating the present value of future cash flows using a discount rate. The discount rate is typically based on the company's cost of capital, which is the weighted average cost of its debt and equity.
