## Risk Factors
Investing in biotechnology companies like Scribe Therapeutics comes with inherent risks. These include the uncertainty of clinical trial outcomes, regulatory hurdles, competition from established players, and the high cost of research and development. Additionally, the CRISPR technology, while promising, is still evolving, and its long-term safety and efficacy are under continuous evaluation.

### Clinical Trial Risks
The success of Scribe Therapeutics' product pipeline is heavily dependent on the outcomes of its clinical trials. Failure in any of these trials can significantly impact the company's stock price and future funding capabilities. Investors must carefully assess the design of these trials, the patient enrollment rates, and any interim data released to gauge the likelihood of success.

### Regulatory Environment
The regulatory landscape for gene editing technologies is complex and varies by jurisdiction. Scribe Therapeutics must navigate these regulations to secure approvals for its therapies. Changes in regulatory policies or unexpected delays in the approval process can pose significant risks to the company's growth plans.

## Competitive Landscape
