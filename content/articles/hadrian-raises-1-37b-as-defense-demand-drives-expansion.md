### Historical Context: Defense Spending Trends
To understand the significance of Hadrian's raise, it's essential to examine historical defense spending trends. Over the past decade, global defense spending has experienced a steady increase, with some fluctuations. The COVID-19 pandemic led to a brief decline in 2020, but the sector has since rebounded, with many countries increasing their defense budgets to address emerging threats.
