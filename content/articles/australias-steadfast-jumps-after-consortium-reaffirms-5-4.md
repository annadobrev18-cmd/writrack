The acquisition of Steadfast by the consortium will be subject to regulatory approvals, including those from the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) and the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA). The regulatory review process will examine the potential impact of the acquisition on competition in the Australian insurance market and the financial stability of the combined entity.

#### Integration Challenges
Post-acquisition, the consortium will face the task of integrating Steadfast into their existing operations.
