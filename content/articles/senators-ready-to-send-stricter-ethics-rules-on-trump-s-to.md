#### Key Provisions of the Clarity Act
The Clarity Act proposes the following key provisions:
* Definition of a cryptocurrency: The act defines a cryptocurrency as a digital asset that uses cryptography for security and is decentralized.
* Regulatory framework: The act proposes that cryptocurrencies should be regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC).
* Disclosure requirements: The act requires cryptocurrency companies to disclose certain information, such as their business model and risk factors.
