## ConocoPhillips (COP) Stock Decline: An Overview
ConocoPhillips (COP), a multinational energy corporation, has witnessed a significant decline in its stock price following the recent US-Iran accord. The agreement, aimed at reviving the 2015 nuclear deal, has led to a surge in Iranian oil exports, thereby increasing global oil supply and exerting downward pressure on oil prices. As a result, COP's stock has been adversely affected, prompting investors to reassess their positions in the energy sector.

### Fundamentals of ConocoPhillips (COP)
To understand the implications of the US-Iran accord on COP's stock, it is essential to examine the company's fundamentals. ConocoPhillips is one of the largest independent oil and gas companies in the world, with operations in over 15 countries. The company's portfolio includes a mix of conventional and unconventional assets, with a focus on oil and natural gas production.
