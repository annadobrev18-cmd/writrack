## The Rise of AI Deepfakes and the Need for Countermeasures
The advent of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has brought about numerous innovations, transforming industries and revolutionizing the way we live and interact. However, one of the most significant challenges posed by AI is the creation and dissemination of deepfakes. These sophisticated, AI-generated content pieces, including videos, audios, and images, have the potential to deceive even the most discerning eye, posing significant threats to personal security, national security, and the integrity of information.

### Historical Context of Deepfakes
The concept of deepfakes is not new, but the technology has advanced significantly over the past few years. The first deepfake videos emerged around 2017, primarily used for entertainment purposes, such as placing actors' faces on other bodies. However, the technology quickly evolved, and by 2020, deepfakes had become a tool for misinformation and fraud. The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated the development and spread of deepfakes, as more people spent time online and the demand for digital content increased.
