With a rich history dating back to 1914, MSA Safety has established itself as a trusted brand in the safety industry.

### Insider Selling: What Does it Mean?

Insider selling can be a significant indicator of a company's future performance. When a high-ranking executive like the chairman sells a substantial amount of shares, it can raise concerns among investors. There are several possible reasons for insider selling, including:

* **Personal financial needs**: The executive may need to raise capital for personal reasons, such as paying off debts or funding a major purchase.
* **Lack of confidence**: The executive may be selling shares due to a lack of confidence in the company's future prospects.
* **Market timing**: The executive may be taking advantage of a high stock price to sell shares before a potential decline.

However, it's essential to note that insider selling can also be a normal part of an executive's compensation package. Many executives receive stock options or restricted stock units as part of their compensation, which they may sell to realize gains or diversify their portfolios.
