## Fundamentals of Maple Leaf Foods
Maple Leaf Foods is a leading Canadian consumer packaged meats company, with a diverse portfolio of brands and products. The company's financial performance is a key indicator of its success and position in the market. In the Q2 2026 earnings call, Maple Leaf Foods posted steady results, with net sales of $1.1 billion, a 2.5% increase from the same period last year.

### Revenue Breakdown
The revenue growth was driven by a 3.2% increase in the Meat Products Group, which includes the company's flagship brands such as Maple Leaf and Schneider's. The Plant-Based Protein Group also saw a significant increase of 10.1% in revenue, driven by the growing demand for plant-based products.
