### Market Impact and Technical Analysis
The earnings beats have not only eased concerns over the record U.S. stock rally but have also provided a technical boost to the market. From a technical analysis perspective, the market has been trading in a bullish trend, with key indices making new highs. The earnings beats have helped to reinforce this trend, as they have provided the necessary momentum for the market to continue its upward trajectory. The Relative Strength Index (RSI) and the Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) are two technical indicators that have been particularly useful in gauging the market's momentum.

- **RSI**: The RSI for the S&P 500 has been oscillating between the overbought and neutral zones, indicating periods of consolidation and potential for further upside.
- **MACD**: The MACD has been positive, with the signal line above the MACD line, suggesting a bullish trend.
