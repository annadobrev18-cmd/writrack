## Frequently Asked Questions
1. **What role does Elon Musk play in Tesla's stock price movements?**
   - Elon Musk, as the CEO and a significant shareholder, has a profound influence on Tesla's stock price. His tweets and public statements can move the market, reflecting the market's perception of his vision and leadership.
2. **How does the global transition to electric vehicles impact Tesla's stock?**
   - The global transition to electric vehicles is a significant tailwind for Tesla, as it increases demand for its products and validates its business model. However, it also attracts more competitors, which can impact Tesla's market share and stock price.
3. **What are the key risks facing Tesla and its investors?**
   - Key risks include production challenges, regulatory changes, competition from traditional automotive manufacturers, and the volatility of the stock market itself. Additionally, any negative news or setbacks in areas like autonomous driving technology or battery safety can impact investor confidence.
