## The Growing Concern of Water Scarcity in AI Data Centers

The rapid growth of the artificial intelligence (AI) industry has led to an increased demand for data centers to process and store vast amounts of data. However, this growth has also raised concerns about the environmental impact of these facilities, particularly with regards to water usage. Data centers require significant amounts of water to cool their systems, which has become a major issue in areas where water is already scarce.
