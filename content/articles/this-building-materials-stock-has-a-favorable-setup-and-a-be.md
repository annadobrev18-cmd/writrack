### Risk Factors
While Owens Corning's financial performance and valuation metrics are attractive, there are several risk factors that investors should consider. These include:

* **Economic Downturn**: A decline in the global economy could lead to a decrease in demand for building materials, negatively impacting Owens Corning's revenue and profitability.
* **Competition**: The building materials sector is highly competitive, with several large players competing for market share. Owens Corning may face challenges in maintaining its market share and pricing power.
* **Regulatory Risks**: Changes in regulations and laws, particularly those related to environmental and safety standards, could increase Owens Corning's costs and negatively impact its profitability.
