## Crypto Clarity Act: A Brief Overview
The Crypto Clarity Act, a bill aimed at providing regulatory clarity for the cryptocurrency industry, has been delayed by the Senate until after its summer break. This move has sparked uncertainty in the crypto market, with investors and industry stakeholders eagerly awaiting the outcome.

### Historical Context: Cryptocurrency Regulation
The cryptocurrency industry has long been plagued by regulatory uncertainty, with different government agencies and regulatory bodies providing conflicting guidance on the classification and treatment of digital assets. The lack of clear regulations has hindered the growth of the industry, making it difficult for companies to operate and for investors to navigate the market.
