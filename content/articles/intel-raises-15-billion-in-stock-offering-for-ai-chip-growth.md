### Historical Context: Intel's Transformation

Intel's decision to raise capital through a stock offering is not unprecedented. In the past, the company has taken similar steps to fund its growth initiatives. For instance, in 2015, Intel acquired Altera, a leading provider of field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs), for $16.7 billion. This acquisition marked a significant milestone in Intel's transformation into a more diversified technology company.

| Acquisition | Year | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Altera | 2015 | $16.7 billion |
| Mobileye | 2017 | $15.3 billion |
| Habana Labs | 2019 | $2 billion |

The table above highlights Intel's recent acquisitions, demonstrating the company's commitment to expanding its presence in the AI and autonomous driving markets.

### Growth Strategy: AI Chip Development

Intel's growth strategy is centered around the development of AI chips, which are designed to accelerate machine learning workloads. The company has made significant progress in this area, with the launch of its Nervana Neural Stick and Movidius Myriad X chips. These products have been well-received by customers, and Intel is poised to benefit from the growing demand for AI-powered technologies.
