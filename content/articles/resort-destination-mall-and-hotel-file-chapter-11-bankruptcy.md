## Risk Factors
Several risk factors are associated with the bankruptcy filing of the resort hotel and mall:

- **Decline in Tourism**: Continued low levels of tourism due to health concerns or economic downturns could hinder recovery efforts.

- **Liquidity Risks**: The need for immediate cash flow to maintain operations while navigating bankruptcy proceedings poses a significant challenge.
