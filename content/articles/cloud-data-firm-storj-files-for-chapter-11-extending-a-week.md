## Overview of Storj and Its Recent Filing
Storj, a cloud data firm, has recently filed for Chapter 11, marking a significant downturn in the crypto market. This move comes after a series of crypto failures, further destabilizing investor confidence. The Storj token has slid 16% following the announcement, reflecting the market's immediate reaction to the news.

### Historical Context of Storj
Storj was founded with the aim of providing decentralized cloud storage solutions. It leveraged blockchain technology to create a network where users could rent out their excess storage space, thereby creating a decentralized alternative to traditional cloud storage providers like Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage. The project garnered significant attention and investment in its early stages due to its innovative approach to data storage and management.
