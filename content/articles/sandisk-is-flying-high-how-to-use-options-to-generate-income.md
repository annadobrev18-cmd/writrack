## Options Trading: A Strategy for Income Generation
For traders looking to capitalize on Sandisk's momentum, options trading presents an attractive opportunity for income generation. By selling options, traders can collect premiums from buyers, providing a regular stream of income. One popular options trading strategy is the covered call, which involves selling call options on a stock that the trader already owns. This strategy allows traders to generate income from the premiums collected, while also benefiting from any potential upside in the stock price.
