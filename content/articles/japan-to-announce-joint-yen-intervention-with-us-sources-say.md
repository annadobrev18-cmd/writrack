### Historical Context: Yen Interventions
Japan has a long history of intervening in the currency market to influence the value of the yen. The most notable example is the 1995 yen intervention, where the Japanese government sold a significant amount of yen to appreciate its value. However, this move ultimately failed, and the yen continued to decline. Since then, Japan has been cautious in its approach to currency intervention.
