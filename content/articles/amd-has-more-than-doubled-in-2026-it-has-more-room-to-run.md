## AMD's Recent Rally: A Closer Look
Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) has been on a tear in 2026, with its stock price more than doubling year-to-date. This impressive rally has been driven by a combination of factors, including the company's promising updates at its AI event earlier this week. According to UBS, AMD looks poised to continue its upward momentum, with the Swiss bank predicting further growth for the chipmaker.

### UBS's Bullish Stance
UBS's optimism towards AMD is rooted in the company's strong fundamentals and growth prospects. The bank's analysts believe that AMD's recent updates, particularly those related to its artificial intelligence (AI) initiatives, have significant potential to drive long-term growth. With the global AI market expected to reach $190 billion by 2025, AMD is well-positioned to capitalize on this trend.

Several key drivers are expected to contribute to AMD's continued growth. These include:
* **Increasing demand for AI-related products**: As AI adoption becomes more widespread, the demand for specialized hardware and software is expected to surge. AMD's AI-focused products, such as its Instinct accelerators, are well-positioned to benefit from this trend.
* **Expanding market share**: AMD has been gaining market share in the CPU and GPU markets, driven by its competitive product offerings and strategic partnerships. This trend is expected to continue, with UBS predicting further share gains for the company.
* **Improving profitability**: AMD's profitability has been improving in recent quarters, driven by its focus on high-margin products and operational efficiencies. This trend is expected to continue, with UBS predicting further margin expansion for the company.
