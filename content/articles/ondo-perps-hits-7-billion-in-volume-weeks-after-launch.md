### Understanding Crypto Derivatives
Before diving into the specifics of Ondo Perps' success, it's essential to understand what crypto derivatives are. Crypto derivatives are financial instruments that derive their value from an underlying cryptocurrency or asset. They allow traders to bet on the future price movements of cryptocurrencies without actually owning the assets. This market has seen significant growth in recent years, with various platforms offering a range of derivative products such as futures, options, and perpetual contracts (perps).
