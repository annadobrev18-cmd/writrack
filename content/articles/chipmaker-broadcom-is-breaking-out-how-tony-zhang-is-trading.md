### Historical Context: Broadcom's Rise to Prominence

To understand the significance of Broadcom's breakout, it's essential to examine the company's history. Founded in 1991, Broadcom has evolved from a small semiconductor company to a global leader in the tech industry. The company's strategic acquisitions, including its purchase of CA Technologies in 2018, have expanded its product portfolio and reinforced its position in the market.

#### Key Milestones:

* 1991: Broadcom is founded by Henry Samueli and Henry T. Nicholas III
* 1998: Broadcom goes public with an initial public offering (IPO)
* 2018: Broadcom acquires CA Technologies for $18.9 billion
* 2020: Broadcom announces its entry into the AI semiconductor market
