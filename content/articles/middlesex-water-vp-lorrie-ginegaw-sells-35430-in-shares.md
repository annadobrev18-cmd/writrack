### Insider Trading: What Does it Mean?
Insider trading refers to the buying or selling of a company's securities by its directors, officers, or employees. These individuals have access to confidential information about the company's operations, financials, and future plans. As such, their trading activities can be seen as a proxy for their confidence in the company's prospects. When an insider sells shares, it may indicate a lack of confidence in the company's short-term prospects or a desire to diversify their portfolio.
