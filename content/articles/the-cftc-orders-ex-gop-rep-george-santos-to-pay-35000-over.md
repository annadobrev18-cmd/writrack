### Background on Kalshi Event Contracts

Kalshi is a platform that offers event contracts, which are a type of financial derivative that allows users to bet on the outcome of specific events. These contracts are considered swaps and are subject to regulation by the CFTC. The CFTC's order against George Santos suggests that he may have violated rules related to trading on these contracts.

### CFTC's Regulatory Framework

The CFTC has a comprehensive regulatory framework in place to oversee trading on event contracts and other derivatives. This framework includes rules related to registration, disclosure, and trading practices. The CFTC also has the authority to investigate and enforce compliance with these rules.

#### Key Provisions of the CFTC's Regulatory Framework

* Registration requirements for platforms offering event contracts
* Disclosure requirements for traders and platforms
* Trading practice rules, including prohibitions on insider trading and market manipulation
