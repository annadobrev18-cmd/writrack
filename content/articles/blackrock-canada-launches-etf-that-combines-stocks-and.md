## Investment Strategy and Rationale
The rationale behind combining stocks and Bitcoin in a single ETF is multifaceted. On one hand, stocks provide a foundation of stability and dividend income, characteristics that are appealing to investors seeking steady returns. On the other hand, Bitcoin offers the potential for high growth, albeit with higher volatility. This combination can potentially mitigate risk while enhancing the portfolio's growth prospects.

### Diversification Benefits
Diversification is a cornerstone of investment strategy, aiming to reduce risk by spreading investments across different asset classes. The inclusion of Bitcoin in an ETF alongside traditional stocks achieves this by introducing an asset that has historically shown low correlation with the stock market. This means that when stocks are performing poorly, Bitcoin might continue to rise, and vice versa, thus smoothing out the overall portfolio performance.

#### Sector Rotation and Market Trends
Investors often engage in sector rotation, moving their investments between different sectors or asset classes based on market trends and economic conditions. The ability to combine stocks and Bitcoin in one ETF simplifies this process, allowing for more agile responses to changing market conditions without the need to manage multiple investment vehicles.
