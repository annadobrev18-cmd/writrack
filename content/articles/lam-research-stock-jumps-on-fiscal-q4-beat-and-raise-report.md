## Fiscal Q4 Earnings Report: A Beat-and-Raise Scenario
Lam Research, a leading player in the semiconductor industry, has reported its fiscal Q4 earnings, surpassing analyst expectations and raising its guidance for the upcoming quarter. The company's stock has responded positively to the news, jumping significantly in after-hours trading.

### Key Highlights of the Earnings Report
The fiscal Q4 earnings report revealed several key highlights, including:
* Revenue of $4.33 billion, exceeding the consensus estimate of $4.23 billion
* Non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) of $7.49, beating the expected $7.17
* Gross margin of 46.1%, up from 45.4% in the previous quarter
* Operating margin of 28.1%, expanding from 26.6% in the prior quarter
