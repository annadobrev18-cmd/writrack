## Microsoft's Short Interest: A Decade High
Microsoft's short interest has reached a decade high ahead of its Q2 earnings report, raising new questions about the company's future prospects. The lack of short covering ahead of the earnings report suggests that bearish investors are holding their positions, anticipating a potential downturn in the company's stock price.

### Historical Context
To understand the significance of Microsoft's short interest, it's essential to examine the historical context. The company's short interest has been increasing steadily over the past year, with a notable spike in the last quarter. This surge in short interest can be attributed to various factors, including the company's significant investments in AI research and development.
