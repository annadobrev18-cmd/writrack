## Social Security Changes: An Overview
The Social Security system is undergoing significant changes that will impact boomers in 2027. ### Change 1: Increase in Full Retirement Age
The full retirement age is the age at which an individual can claim their full Social Security benefits without any reduction. In 2027, the full retirement age is set to increase to 67 years old for individuals born in 1960 or later. This change will impact boomers who were born between 1946 and 1954, as they will need to work for an additional year to claim their full benefits.

### Change 2: Reduction in Cost-of-Living Adjustments (COLAs)
The COLA is an annual increase in Social Security benefits to keep pace with inflation. In 2027, the COLA is set to be reduced, which means that Social Security benefits will not keep pace with inflation. This change will impact boomers who are already receiving Social Security benefits, as their benefits will not increase at the same rate as inflation.

#### Impact on Boomers
The reduction in COLAs will have a significant impact on boomers who are relying on Social Security benefits as a major source of retirement income. As inflation increases, the purchasing power of their benefits will decrease, which could result in a reduction in their standard of living.

### Change 3: Increase in Medicare Premiums
Medicare premiums are set to increase in 2027, which will impact boomers who are enrolled in Medicare. The increase in premiums will be significant, with some estimates suggesting that premiums could increase by as much as 10%.

#### Impact on Boomers
The increase in Medicare premiums will have a significant impact on boomers who are enrolled in Medicare. Many boomers may not be able to afford the increase in premiums, which could result in a reduction in their healthcare coverage.
