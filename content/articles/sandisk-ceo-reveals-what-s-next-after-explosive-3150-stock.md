#### Key Milestones in SanDisk's Growth
- **1988**: SanDisk was founded by Eli Harari, Sanjay Mehrotra, and Jack Yuan.
- **1995**: The company went public with its initial public offering (IPO).
- **2011**: SanDisk acquired Pliant Technology, expanding its SSD portfolio.
- **2014**: The company purchased Fusion-io, further strengthening its position in the SSD market.
- **2015**: Western Digital acquired SanDisk for approximately $19 billion, marking a significant milestone in the company's history.
