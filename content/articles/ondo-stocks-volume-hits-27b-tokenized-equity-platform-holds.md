### Historical Context of Tokenized Equity
Tokenized equity refers to the process of issuing digital tokens that represent ownership in a company. This concept has been around for several years but has gained significant traction in recent times due to advancements in blockchain technology and regulatory clarity. The first tokenized equity offerings were seen in the early 2010s, but it wasn't until the mid-2010s that the concept started gaining mainstream attention.
