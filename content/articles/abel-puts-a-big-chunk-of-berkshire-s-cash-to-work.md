## Berkshire's Cash Conundrum
Berkshire Hathaway, the multinational conglomerate led by Greg Abel, has long been known for its vast cash reserves. ### Historical Context

However, with the changing market landscape and the transition to new leadership, it was unclear how Abel would approach the company's cash management strategy.
