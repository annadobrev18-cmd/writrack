### Risk Factors: Challenges Facing Huntington Bancshares

Despite its strong fundamentals and competitive valuation, Huntington Bancshares faces several risk factors that could impact its performance and Wall Street's sentiment. These include:

- **Economic Downturn**: A decline in economic activity could lead to decreased loan demand and higher credit losses.
- **Regulatory Changes**: Stricter regulations or changes in monetary policy could increase operational costs and reduce profitability.
- **Competition**: The banking sector is highly competitive, with Huntington Bancshares facing challenges from both traditional banks and fintech companies.
