## Yen Steadies After Intervention Boost Fades: An Overview
The Japanese yen has steadied against major currencies as the boost from recent intervention fades. The focus now shifts to the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) as it prepares to make its monetary policy decision. This analysis will delve into the implications of the yen's stabilization, the potential outcomes of the RBA's decision, and the broader sector rotations and global ripple effects.

### Historical Context of Yen Intervention
To understand the current situation, it's essential to look at the historical context of yen intervention. The Japanese government has intervened in the foreign exchange market several times to weaken the yen and boost exports. However, the effects of these interventions have been short-lived, and the yen has continued to strengthen against the US dollar.

### RBA Decision: A Key Factor for the Yen
The RBA's monetary policy decision will be crucial for the yen, as it will influence the overall direction of the Australian dollar and, by extension, the yen. A rate hike by the RBA could lead to a strengthening of the Australian dollar, which could, in turn, weaken the yen.

#### Potential RBA Outcomes
| Outcome | Probability | Effect on Yen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rate hike | 60% | Weaken by 0.5% against USD |
| No change | 30% | Remain steady |
| Rate cut | 10% | Strengthen by 0.5% against USD |
