## Russia's Largest Bank Enters the Crypto Space
Sberbank, Russia's largest bank, has announced plans to establish a crypto trading infrastructure by December. This move marks a significant milestone in the country's growing acceptance of digital assets. As a major financial institution, Sberbank's entry into the crypto space is expected to bring increased legitimacy and stability to the market.

### Historical Context: Russia's Crypto Regulatory Environment
Russia has had a complex relationship with cryptocurrencies in the past. In 2020, the country introduced the Digital Financial Assets (DFA) law, which recognized cryptocurrencies as property but prohibited their use as a means of payment. However, in recent years, the Russian government has shown a growing interest in the potential of digital assets. The country's central bank, the Bank of Russia, has been exploring the development of a central bank digital currency (CBDC) and has also been involved in discussions around the regulation of cryptocurrencies.
