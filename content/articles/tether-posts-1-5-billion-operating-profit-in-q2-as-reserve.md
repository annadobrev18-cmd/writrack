#### Reserve Buffer: A Key Metric for Stablecoins
The reserve buffer is a critical metric for stablecoins, representing the percentage of assets held in reserve to back the stablecoin's value. Tether's reserve buffer has fallen by half in Q2, raising concerns among investors and analysts about the company's ability to maintain the stablecoin's peg to the US dollar.
