## Standard Chartered's Venture into Stablecoins
The recent launch of the Hong Kong dollar stablecoin by Standard Chartered-led Anchorpoint marks a significant milestone in the financial sector. This move is expected to have far-reaching implications for the cryptocurrency market, traditional banking, and the broader economy. ### Fundamentals of Stablecoins
Stablecoins are a type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value relative to a fiat currency, in this case, the Hong Kong dollar. They are typically collateralized by a reserve of the underlying asset, which in this instance, would be the Hong Kong dollar. The primary purpose of stablecoins is to provide a low-volatility store of value and a medium of exchange, mitigating the price fluctuations inherent in other cryptocurrencies.

#### How Stablecoins Work
Stablecoins operate by maintaining a peg to the underlying asset through a combination of algorithms, reserve management, and market forces. The issuer of the stablecoin, in this case, Anchorpoint, is responsible for maintaining the reserve and ensuring that the stablecoin's value remains stable. This is achieved through a process of buying and selling the stablecoin on the market, as well as adjusting the supply of stablecoins in circulation.
