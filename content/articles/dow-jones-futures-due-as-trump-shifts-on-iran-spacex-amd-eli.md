As investors, it's crucial to understand the implications of such developments on the stock market, especially considering the upcoming earnings reports from key players like SpaceX, AMD, Sandisk, and Eli Lilly.

#### Impact on Oil Prices
One of the immediate effects of the increased tensions with Iran has been the rise in oil prices. As a major oil producer, any disruption to Iranian oil exports could lead to a shortage in global supplies, driving up prices. This, in turn, could have a ripple effect on the economy, impacting inflation, consumer spending, and ultimately, corporate earnings.

## Upcoming Earnings Reports
The upcoming earnings reports from SpaceX, AMD, Sandisk, and Eli Lilly will be closely watched by investors, as they could provide insight into the health of their respective industries and the broader economy.

### SpaceX Earnings
SpaceX, a private company, does not release its financial statements publicly. However, any announcements or updates on its operations and future plans could have an impact on the stock prices of related companies or the overall space industry.

### AMD Earnings
AMD is expected to report its quarterly earnings, with investors looking for signs of continued growth in the semiconductor industry. The company has been gaining market share from its competitor, Intel, and any positive earnings report could lead to an increase in its stock price.

### Sandisk Earnings
Sandisk, a leading manufacturer of flash memory products, is also due to report its earnings. The company's performance will be closely watched, given the increasing demand for data storage solutions.

### Eli Lilly Earnings
Eli Lilly, a major pharmaceutical company, will report its quarterly earnings, with investors looking for updates on its pipeline of new drugs and any potential impact from changes in the healthcare industry.
