Several factors contribute to the stall in Hyperliquid ETF inflows. One primary reason is the increasing competition in the market. As more financial institutions and investment firms launch their own Hyperliquid ETFs, the market becomes increasingly crowded. This competition leads to a decrease in demand for existing ETFs, resulting in stalled inflows.
