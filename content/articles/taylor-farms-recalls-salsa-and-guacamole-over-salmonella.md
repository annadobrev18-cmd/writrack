Taylor Farms is a leading producer of fresh produce, including salads, vegetables, and fruits. The company has a strong presence in the US market, with its products being sold in major retailers such as Walmart, Kroger, and Whole Foods. ### The Recall: What Happened?
According to reports, Taylor Farms initiated the recall after detecting salmonella in some of its salsa and guacamole products. The affected products were distributed to retailers across the US, and consumers have been advised to return or discard them. The recall is a precautionary measure to prevent any potential harm to consumers.

### Financial Impact on Taylor Farms
The recall is likely to have a significant financial impact on Taylor Farms. The company will incur costs associated with the recall, including the cost of retrieving and destroying the affected products, as well as potential legal fees. Additionally, the recall may lead to a decline in sales and revenue, as consumers may lose confidence in the company's products.
