## Alphabet's Q2 Earnings: A Miss on Spending Forecast
Alphabet, the parent company of Google, released its second-quarter earnings, which fell short of expectations due to a significant increase in spending forecast. The company's stock price took a hit, prompting several analysts to lower their price targets. In this analysis, we will delve into the details of Alphabet's Q2 earnings, the historical context of the company's spending, and the potential impact on the stock market.

The company's investments in research and development, marketing, and capital expenditures have been a key driver of its growth. However, the recent increase in spending forecast has raised concerns among investors. To put this into perspective, let's examine Alphabet's spending trends over the past few years.

### Market Impact: Analyst Reactions
The increase in spending forecast has led to a decrease in Alphabet's stock price. Several analysts have lowered their price targets, citing concerns about the company's ability to maintain profitability. Here are some reactions from Wall Street analysts:

* Morgan Stanley: Lowered price target from $140 to $120, citing concerns about the company's spending growth.
* Goldman Sachs: Reduced price target from $150 to $130, noting that the increase in spending forecast could impact profitability.
* J.P. Morgan: Lowered price target from $145 to $125, stating that the company's spending growth is a concern.
