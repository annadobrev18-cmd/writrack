## Bitcoin's Current Dilemma
The cryptocurrency market, particularly bitcoin, has been under intense scrutiny lately. ### Historical Context
Bitcoin has experienced significant fluctuations in its value over the years. From its inception in 2009, the cryptocurrency has witnessed numerous bull and bear cycles. The most notable bull run occurred in 2020-2021, where the price of bitcoin skyrocketed to an all-time high of nearly $65,000. However, the market has been correcting since then, with the price currently hovering around $30,000.
