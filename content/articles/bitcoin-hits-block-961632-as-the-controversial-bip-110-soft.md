### Historical Context: Understanding BIP-110

To grasp the significance of BIP-110, it's essential to delve into the history of Bitcoin's development. The Bitcoin Improvement Proposal (BIP) process allows developers to suggest changes to the protocol, which are then reviewed and debated by the community. BIP-110, in particular, aims to introduce a new consensus mechanism, altering the way transactions are validated and added to the blockchain.

The proposal has been met with skepticism by some, who argue that the changes could potentially compromise the security and decentralization of the network. Others see BIP-110 as a necessary step towards improving the scalability and efficiency of the blockchain. The fact that less than 3% of miners are currently supporting the proposal highlights the divisions within the community.
