### Background on Bhutan's GMC
Bhutan's GMC, or Gross National Happiness Commission, is a government agency responsible for promoting the country's unique development philosophy, which prioritizes happiness and well-being over economic growth. The agency has been exploring innovative ways to diversify the country's economy and attract foreign investment. The recent bitcoin pledge is a significant step in this direction.
