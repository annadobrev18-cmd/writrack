#### Tesla (TSLA)
Tesla, the electric vehicle (EV) pioneer, has been one of the most hyped stocks in recent years. However, Burry has been skeptical of the company's valuation, which has been driven largely by speculation and momentum rather than fundamental analysis. With a price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of over 100, Tesla's stock has been ripe for a correction.

#### Caterpillar (CAT)
Caterpillar, the heavy machinery manufacturer, has been benefiting from the global infrastructure boom. However, Burry may be concerned about the company's exposure to the highly cyclical construction industry, which is prone to downturns. Additionally, Caterpillar's stock has been trading at a premium to its historical averages, making it vulnerable to a pullback.

#### Nvidia (NVDA)
Nvidia, the graphics processing unit (GPU) specialist, has been a darling of the tech sector in recent years. However, Burry may be worried about the company's dependence on the highly competitive and rapidly evolving tech industry. With a P/E ratio of over 50, Nvidia's stock has been trading at a significant premium to its peers, making it a potential short candidate.

#### Applied Materials (AMAT)
Applied Materials, the semiconductor equipment manufacturer, has been benefiting from the global chip shortage. However, Burry may be concerned about the company's exposure to the highly cyclical semiconductor industry, which is prone to downturns. Additionally, Applied Materials' stock has been trading at a premium to its historical averages, making it vulnerable to a pullback.
