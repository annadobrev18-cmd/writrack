## Crypto Stocks Defy Broader Market Trend

The stock market saw a significant sell-off on Monday, but crypto stocks managed to buck the trend, posting some of the day's top gains. This unexpected rally was largely driven by a rotation in capital away from chip and AI infrastructure names. As investors reassessed their portfolios, they seemed to find value in the crypto sector, which has been under pressure in recent months.

### Rotation from AI Infrastructure

The AI infrastructure sector has been one of the hottest areas of the market in recent years, with stocks like NVIDIA and AMD leading the charge. However, as the sector's valuations reached lofty heights, investors began to look for alternative places to put their money. Crypto stocks, which have been lagging behind the broader market, became an attractive option.

#### Sector Comparison

| Sector | 1-Day Return | 1-Week Return | 1-Month Return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crypto Stocks | 5.2% | 10.1% | 15.6% |
| AI Infrastructure | -2.5% | -5.1% | -10.3% |
| Broader Market | -1.1% | -2.5% | -5.5% |

As the table above shows, crypto stocks have significantly outperformed the AI infrastructure sector and the broader market over the past month. This rotation in capital is a significant development, as it suggests that investors are becoming more cautious about the valuations of AI infrastructure stocks.

### Bitcoin Miners Lag Behind

While crypto stocks as a whole saw a significant rally, bitcoin miners lagged behind. This is likely due to the fact that bitcoin miners are heavily dependent on the price of bitcoin, which has been relatively stable in recent weeks. As a result, investors may be less enthusiastic about bitcoin miners, which are seen as more speculative and volatile.
