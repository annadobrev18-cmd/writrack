### What is BIP-110?

BIP-110, or Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 110, is a proposal aimed at improving the bitcoin network's scalability and efficiency. It seeks to introduce a new transaction format that would allow for more efficient processing of transactions, thereby increasing the network's capacity. The proposal has been met with both support and criticism from various stakeholders, including miners, developers, and users.
