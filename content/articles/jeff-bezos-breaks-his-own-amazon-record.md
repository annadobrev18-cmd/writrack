### Historical Context: Amazon's Rise to Prominence

To appreciate the significance of Bezos' achievement, it's essential to understand Amazon's history. Founded in 1994, Amazon started as an online bookstore but quickly expanded into other product categories, becoming one of the world's largest e-commerce platforms. Over the years, Amazon has diversified its operations, venturing into cloud computing (AWS), artificial intelligence, and even physical retail. This strategic expansion has enabled Amazon to stay ahead of the competition and adapt to changing market trends.

#### Key Milestones in Amazon's History

- 1997: Amazon goes public with an initial public offering (IPO) that raises $54 million.
- 2002: Amazon launches Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), a service that allows third-party sellers to store and ship products from Amazon's warehouses.
- 2006: Amazon Web Services (AWS) is launched, providing cloud computing services to businesses and individuals.
- 2011: Amazon launches its Prime membership program, offering free two-day shipping, streaming services, and other benefits.
- 2014: Amazon acquires Twitch, a live streaming platform for video games, for $970 million.
- 2017: Amazon acquires Whole Foods Market, a chain of upscale grocery stores, for $13.7 billion.
