### Airtable: A Valuable Asset
Airtable, on the other hand, is a cloud-based database and project management tool that has gained significant traction in recent years. With a user base of over 200,000 organizations, Airtable has become a go-to solution for companies looking to streamline their workflow and improve collaboration. The company's valuation prior to the acquisition was estimated to be around $11.7 billion, making the $9 billion discount a significant coup for Bending Spoons.

### Rationale Behind the Deal
So, what drove Bending Spoons to acquire Airtable at a discounted price? One possible reason is the company's desire to expand its offerings and provide a more comprehensive solution to its customers. By integrating Airtable's project management capabilities with its own software development expertise, Bending Spoons can offer a unique value proposition that sets it apart from competitors.

#### Synergies and Cost Savings
The acquisition is expected to generate significant synergies and cost savings for Bending Spoons.
