## Edwards Lifesciences: The 'Cleanest Large-Cap Medtech Story'
Edwards Lifesciences, a leading medical technology company, has been dubbed the 'cleanest large-cap medtech story' due to its strong financial performance and growth prospects. The company recently reported its Q2 2026 earnings, which beat analysts' expectations. However, the stock price reversed after the announcement, leaving investors wondering what's next for Edwards Lifesciences.

### Historical Context
Edwards Lifesciences has a long history of innovation and growth. The company was founded in 1958 and has since become a leader in the development and manufacture of medical devices and equipment. Edwards Lifesciences' product portfolio includes heart valves, cardiac surgery systems, and critical care monitoring devices. The company has a strong track record of delivering consistent revenue growth and expanding its profit margins.
