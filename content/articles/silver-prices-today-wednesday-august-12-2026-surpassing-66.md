## Fundamentals of Silver Prices
The price of silver has been on a tear recently, surpassing $66 per ounce as of Wednesday, August 12, 2026. This surge in price can be attributed to easing expectations for July inflation. The decrease in inflation expectations has led to a decrease in the value of the US dollar, making silver more attractive to investors.

### Historical Context
To understand the current price movement, it's essential to look at the historical context of silver prices. Over the past year, silver prices have been volatile, with prices ranging from $50 to $65 per ounce. The current price of $66 per ounce represents a significant increase from the lows of 2025.
