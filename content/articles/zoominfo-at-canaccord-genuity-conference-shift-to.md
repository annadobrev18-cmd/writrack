### Background on ZoomInfo

ZoomInfo is a cloud-based platform that provides sales and marketing teams with access to a vast database of contact and company information. The company's solutions enable businesses to streamline their sales and marketing efforts, improve conversion rates, and ultimately drive revenue growth. With a strong focus on innovation and customer satisfaction, ZoomInfo has established itself as a leader in the go-to-market intelligence space.

### The Shift to Consumption

The shift to a consumption-based model is a significant strategic move for ZoomInfo. Under this model, customers will be charged based on their actual usage of the platform, rather than being required to pay a fixed subscription fee. This approach is expected to provide customers with greater flexibility and cost savings, while also enabling ZoomInfo to better align its revenue with the value it delivers to its customers.

#### Key Benefits of the Consumption Strategy

The consumption strategy is expected to bring several benefits to ZoomInfo, including:
* Increased customer satisfaction: By charging customers only for what they use, ZoomInfo can help reduce costs and improve the overall customer experience.
* Improved revenue visibility: The consumption model provides ZoomInfo with greater visibility into its revenue streams, enabling the company to better forecast and manage its growth.
* Enhanced competitiveness: The shift to a consumption-based model is expected to make ZoomInfo's solutions more competitive in the market, as customers will be able to choose from a range of pricing options that best fit their needs.
