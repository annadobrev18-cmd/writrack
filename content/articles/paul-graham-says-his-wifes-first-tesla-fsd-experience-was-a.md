## Fundamentals of Tesla's FSD
Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) technology has been making waves in the automotive and AI communities. The recent experience of Paul Graham's wife, which he described as a 'religious revelation,' has further fueled the excitement. But what exactly is FSD, and how does it work?

### Technical Overview
FSD is a advanced driver-assistance system (ADAS) that enables Tesla vehicles to operate autonomously in most situations. The system uses a combination of cameras, radar, ultrasonic sensors, and GPS to navigate roads and respond to traffic conditions. The technology is powered by Tesla's proprietary software, which is continuously updated and improved through over-the-air updates.

## Risk Factors
While Tesla's FSD technology has significant potential, there are also several risk factors that investors should be aware of.

### Regulatory Risks
The regulatory environment for autonomous driving is still evolving, and there is a risk that governments may impose stricter regulations on the technology. This could impact Tesla's ability to deploy and monetize its FSD technology.

### Technical Risks
The development and deployment of FSD technology is complex and requires significant technical expertise. There is a risk that Tesla may encounter technical challenges that could impact the performance and safety of its FSD system.

### Competitive Risks
The autonomous driving market is highly competitive, and there is a risk that other companies may develop more advanced or cost-effective FSD systems. This could impact Tesla's market share and revenue growth.
