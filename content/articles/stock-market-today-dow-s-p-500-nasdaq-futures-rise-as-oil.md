## Current Market Trends
The stock market today saw a significant rise in futures for the Dow, S&P 500, and Nasdaq, as oil prices tumbled. This trend is not unusual, given the historical inverse correlation between oil prices and stock market performance. When oil prices fall, it often leads to increased consumer spending and reduced production costs for companies, thereby boosting stock prices.
