## Exchange Duopoly: A Dominant Force in the Market
The exchange landscape has long been dominated by two major players: CME Group (CME) and Cboe (CBOE). These two entities have consistently demonstrated their ability to thrive in various market conditions, earning them praise from renowned financial experts like Jim Cramer. In this analysis, we will delve into the factors contributing to their success and explore the implications of their dominance on the market.

### Historical Context: The Rise of CME Group and Cboe
To understand the current state of the exchange duopoly, it is essential to examine the historical context of CME Group and Cboe. CME Group, formed in 2002, has its roots in the Chicago Mercantile Exchange, which was founded in 1898. Over the years, CME Group has expanded its operations through strategic acquisitions, including the Chicago Board of Trade (CBOT) and the New York Mercantile Exchange (NYMEX). Cboe, on the other hand, was founded in 1973 as the Chicago Board Options Exchange. It has since grown to become one of the largest options exchanges in the world.
