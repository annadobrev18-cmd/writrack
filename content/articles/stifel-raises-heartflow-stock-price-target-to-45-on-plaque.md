## Stifel's Upward Revision of HeartFlow Stock Price Target
The recent announcement by Stifel, a leading financial services company, to raise the stock price target of HeartFlow to $45 has sent ripples through the investment community. This upward revision is primarily attributed to the significant growth in plaque, a critical factor in the diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. As a result, investors are keenly watching the developments in HeartFlow's stock, anticipating potential gains.

### Background on HeartFlow
HeartFlow is a medical technology company that specializes in developing innovative solutions for cardiovascular disease diagnosis and treatment. The company's flagship product is the HeartFlow FFRct, a non-invasive diagnostic tool that utilizes computational fluid dynamics to analyze coronary artery disease. This technology enables physicians to make more accurate diagnoses and develop personalized treatment plans for patients.
