## Fed's July Interest Rate Decision
The Federal Reserve's July interest rate decision has sent shockwaves through the market, with investors and analysts scrambling to understand the implications of the move. The Fed's decision to raise interest rates by 25 basis points has been seen as a hawkish move, with many expecting a more dovish approach.

### Historical Context
To understand the significance of the Fed's decision, it is essential to look at the historical context. The Fed has been raising interest rates since 2022, in an effort to combat inflation and slow down the economy. The current interest rate hike is the 11th consecutive increase, with the federal funds rate now standing at 5.5%.
