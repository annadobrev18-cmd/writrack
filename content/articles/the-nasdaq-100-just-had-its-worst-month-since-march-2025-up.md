## Nasdaq-100's Worst Month Since March 2025: A Deeper Look
The Nasdaq-100 index just experienced its worst month since March 2025, with a significant decline in its value. However, despite this downturn, eight stocks within the index managed to gain value in July. These stocks not only withstood the market's decline but are also expected to outperform in the future.

These stocks come from various sectors, including technology, healthcare, and consumer goods. Their ability to gain value in a declining market suggests that they have strong fundamentals and are well-positioned for future growth.

### Analysis of the Resilient Stocks
#### Microsoft
Microsoft's gain in July can be attributed to its strong cloud computing business and growing demand for its software products. The company's Azure cloud platform has been gaining market share, and its Office 365 software suite remains a popular choice among businesses and individuals.

#### Alphabet
Alphabet's gain in July was driven by its strong advertising revenue and growing demand for its Google Cloud platform. The company's YouTube video sharing platform also continues to attract new users and advertisers.

#### Amazon
Amazon's gain in July was driven by its strong e-commerce business and growing demand for its cloud computing services. The company's Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud platform is a leader in the market, and its e-commerce platform continues to attract new customers.

#### NVIDIA
NVIDIA's gain in July was driven by its strong gaming and artificial intelligence (AI) businesses. The company's graphics processing units (GPUs) are in high demand for gaming and AI applications, and its recent partnerships with major tech companies have further boosted its growth prospects.

#### Tesla
Tesla's gain in July was driven by its strong electric vehicle sales and growing demand for its energy storage products. The company's Autopilot technology also continues to attract new customers, and its recent partnerships with major automakers have further boosted its growth prospects.

#### UnitedHealth Group
UnitedHealth Group's gain in July was driven by its strong healthcare business and growing demand for its insurance products. The company's Optum health services division also continues to attract new customers, and its recent partnerships with major healthcare providers have further boosted its growth prospects.

#### Cisco Systems
Cisco Systems' gain in July was driven by its strong networking business and growing demand for its cybersecurity products. The company's recent partnerships with major tech companies have further boosted its growth prospects, and its dividend yield remains attractive to income investors.

#### Intel
Intel's gain in July was driven by its strong semiconductor business and growing demand for its AI and autonomous driving products. The company's recent partnerships with major tech companies have further boosted its growth prospects, and its dividend yield remains attractive to income investors.

## Fed Implications and Sector Rotations
The Nasdaq-100's worst month since March 2025 has significant implications for the Federal Reserve's monetary policy. The decline in the index suggests that the economy may be slowing down, which could lead to a decrease in interest rates. This, in turn, could boost the stock market and lead to a rotation into growth sectors such as technology and healthcare.

### Global Ripple Effects
The decline in the Nasdaq-100 index also has global implications. A slowing down of the US economy could lead to a decrease in demand for goods and services from other countries, which could have a negative impact on their economies. However, a decrease in interest rates could also lead to an increase in foreign investment in the US, which could boost the stock market and lead to a rotation into growth sectors.
