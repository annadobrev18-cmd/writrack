### Overview of GlobalFoundries

GlobalFoundries is a semiconductor manufacturing company that was founded in 2009 as a joint venture between Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and the Advanced Technology Investment Company (ATIC). The company has since become one of the largest independent semiconductor foundries in the world, with a global presence and a diverse customer base. GlobalFoundries offers a wide range of semiconductor manufacturing services, including wafer fabrication, assembly, and test.
