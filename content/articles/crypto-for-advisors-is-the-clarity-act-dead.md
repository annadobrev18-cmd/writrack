### Background: The Clarity Act and Its Objectives

The Clarity Act was introduced to address the ambiguity surrounding the regulation of digital assets. Its primary objective was to provide a clear framework for the treatment of cryptocurrencies, ensuring that financial advisors and institutions could operate with confidence. The Act aimed to achieve this by:

* Providing a clear definition of digital assets
* Establishing a regulatory framework for digital asset transactions
* Clarifying the role of regulatory bodies in overseeing digital asset markets
