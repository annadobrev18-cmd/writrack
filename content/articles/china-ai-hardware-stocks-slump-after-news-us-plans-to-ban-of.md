## US-China Tech War: A New Front Opens
The US-China trade war has taken a new turn with the news that the US plans to ban imports of Chinese components, particularly those related to AI hardware. This move has sent shockwaves through the Chinese stock market, with AI hardware stocks suffering significant losses. The ban is seen as a strategic move by the US to limit China's growing influence in the tech sector and to protect its own domestic industries.

### Historical Context: US-China Trade War
The US-China trade war has been ongoing for several years, with both countries imposing tariffs on each other's goods. The conflict has escalated in recent times, with the US taking a more aggressive stance against China's tech sector. The US has been concerned about China's rapid advancement in areas such as AI, 5G, and semiconductors, and has taken steps to limit China's access to US technology and markets.

#### Key Events in the US-China Trade War
* 2018: The US imposes tariffs on $34 billion worth of Chinese goods, including electronics and machinery.
* 2019: China retaliates with tariffs on $60 billion worth of US goods, including agricultural products and energy.
* 2020: The US bans Chinese tech giant Huawei from accessing US technology and markets.
* 2022: The US passes the CHIPS Act, aimed at boosting domestic semiconductor production and reducing reliance on Chinese imports.
