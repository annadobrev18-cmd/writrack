## Geopolitical Tensions and Oil Markets
The recent news of missiles targeting Saudi oil refineries being shot down has significant implications for global oil markets and investors. The attack, allegedly carried out by Iranian-backed Houthi rebels, has heightened tensions in the region and raised concerns about the stability of oil supplies.

### Historical Context
This is not the first time that Saudi oil refineries have been targeted. In 2019, a drone attack on Saudi Aramco's Abqaiq and Khurais oil facilities knocked out half of the country's oil production, causing a significant spike in oil prices. The attack was claimed by the Houthi rebels, but the US and Saudi Arabia blamed Iran, which denied any involvement.
