The recent attack on an ADNOC vessel in the Strait of Hormuz has heightened geopolitical tensions in the region, raising concerns about the safety of oil tankers and the potential impact on global oil markets. This incident, combined with the extended deployment of a US aircraft carrier in the region, has significant implications for investors and market analysts.

### Historical Context: Strait of Hormuz and Global Oil Supply
The Strait of Hormuz is a critical waterway that connects the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, with approximately 20% of the world's oil supply passing through it. The strait is bordered by Iran to the north and the United Arab Emirates and Oman to the south. The region has been a flashpoint for geopolitical tensions, with Iran and the US having a complex and often contentious relationship.
