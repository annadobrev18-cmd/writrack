## Fundamentals of Church & Dwight
Church & Dwight, the renowned manufacturer of Arm & Hammer baking soda, has been garnering attention for its unique profile that can potentially buffer portfolio drawdowns relative to the S&P 500. With a history spanning over 180 years, the company has established itself as a stalwart in the consumer staples sector. Its product portfolio, which includes a wide range of household and personal care items, is designed to meet the everyday needs of consumers, thereby providing a stable source of revenue.

### Historical Performance
A review of Church & Dwight's historical performance reveals a story of steady growth and resilience. The company has consistently delivered solid earnings and dividend growth, making it an attractive option for investors seeking stable returns. Its ability to navigate through economic downturns with minimal disruption to its operations is a testament to its strong business model and diversified product offerings.
