## NorthWestern Corporation Earnings Analysis
NorthWestern Corporation, a leading provider of essential services, including electricity and natural gas, recently announced its earnings, which missed estimates by $0.01. However, the company's revenue topped estimates, indicating a mixed bag for investors. In this analysis, we will delve into the details of the earnings report, examining the key drivers of the company's financial performance and the implications for investors.

### Earnings Per Share (EPS) Analysis
The company reported an EPS of $1.23, missing the consensus estimate of $1.24 by $0.01. This represents a decline of 2.4% from the same quarter last year. The EPS miss can be attributed to higher operating expenses, which increased by 5.6% year-over-year. The higher expenses were largely driven by increased fuel and purchased power costs, which rose by 10.3% year-over-year.

### Revenue Analysis
On the other hand, the company's revenue topped estimates, coming in at $434.8 million, beating the consensus estimate of $429.1 million. This represents a growth of 4.2% from the same quarter last year. The revenue growth was driven by a 3.5% increase in electricity sales and a 5.1% increase in natural gas sales.

### Segment Performance
The company's electricity segment reported revenue of $244.1 million, up 3.2% year-over-year. The natural gas segment reported revenue of $143.4 million, up 5.5% year-over-year. The other segment, which includes the company's unregulated businesses, reported revenue of $47.3 million, up 2.1% year-over-year.
