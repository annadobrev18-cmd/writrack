### Economic Impact Analysis
The economic impact of the Moscow bombing can be analyzed from several angles, including the effect on tourism, trade, and investor confidence.

#### Tourism and Travel
The tourism industry is likely to be one of the sectors most directly affected by the bombing. Moscow, being a significant tourist destination, may experience a decline in visitor numbers, at least in the short term. This could have a ripple effect on the local economy, impacting businesses that rely on tourism, such as hotels, restaurants, and souvenir shops.

#### Trade and Commerce
Geopolitical tensions can also affect trade and commerce. The bombing may lead to increased scrutiny and potential restrictions on trade with Russia, which could have broader implications for global supply chains and economic stability.

#### Investor Confidence
Perhaps the most significant economic impact of the Moscow bombing will be on investor confidence. In times of geopolitical uncertainty, investors often become risk-averse, seeking safer investments and potentially leading to a decrease in market values.
