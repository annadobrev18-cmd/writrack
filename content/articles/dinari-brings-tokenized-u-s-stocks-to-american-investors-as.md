### Fundamentals of Tokenized Stocks

Tokenized stocks are created through a process called tokenization, where a traditional security is converted into a digital token. This token is then stored on a blockchain, ensuring the security, transparency, and immutability of the investment. The tokenization process involves several key players, including the issuer, the investor, and the blockchain platform.

#### Key Players in Tokenization

* **Issuer**: The issuer is the entity responsible for creating the tokenized stock. This can be a company, a financial institution, or a specialized tokenization platform.
* **Investor**: The investor is the individual or institution that purchases the tokenized stock. Investors can buy, sell, and trade tokenized stocks on various platforms.
* **Blockchain Platform**: The blockchain platform is the underlying technology that enables the creation, storage, and trading of tokenized stocks. Blockchain platforms provide a secure, transparent, and efficient way to manage tokenized assets.

### Valuation of Tokenized Stocks

The valuation of tokenized stocks is similar to traditional securities, with the price determined by market forces. However, tokenized stocks offer several advantages, including increased liquidity, reduced transaction costs, and improved accessibility.

#### Advantages of Tokenized Stocks

* **Increased Liquidity**: Tokenized stocks can be traded 24/7, providing investors with greater flexibility and liquidity.
* **Reduced Transaction Costs**: Tokenized stocks eliminate the need for intermediaries, reducing transaction costs and increasing efficiency.
* **Improved Accessibility**: Tokenized stocks can be purchased with cryptocurrencies, making it easier for investors to access the US equity market.

### Risk Factors Associated with Tokenized Stocks

While tokenized stocks offer several advantages, they also come with unique risk factors. Investors should be aware of these risks before investing in tokenized stocks.

#### Risk Factors

* **Regulatory Risks**: The regulatory environment for tokenized stocks is still evolving, and changes in regulations can impact the value of tokenized assets.
* **Security Risks**: Tokenized stocks are stored on blockchain platforms, which can be vulnerable to cyber attacks and security breaches.
* **Market Risks**: Tokenized stocks are subject to market fluctuations, and investors can lose value if the market declines.
