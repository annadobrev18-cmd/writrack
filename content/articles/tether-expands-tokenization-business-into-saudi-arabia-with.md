## Fundamentals of Tokenized Real Estate
Tokenized real estate involves the creation of digital tokens that represent ownership or investment interests in physical properties. This approach offers several benefits, including increased liquidity, reduced transaction costs, and improved accessibility for investors. Tokenization also enables the creation of new financial instruments, such as real estate investment trusts (REITs) and property funds, which can be traded on blockchain networks.
