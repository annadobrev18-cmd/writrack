### Form 144 Filing: What Does it Mean?
A Form 144 filing is a notice of proposed sale of securities by an insider, which can include officers, directors, or significant shareholders. The filing is required by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to ensure transparency and provide investors with insight into potential insider activity. In the case of Associated Banc-Corp, the Form 144 filing indicates that an insider intends to sell a significant number of shares, which can be a cause for concern among investors.
