#### The Rise of Kazakhstan as a Mining Hub
Kazakhstan, in particular, has become a significant player in the Bitcoin mining landscape. The country's cheap electricity, favorable climate, and welcoming regulatory environment have attracted numerous mining operators. According to the Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance, Kazakhstan accounts for approximately 18% of the global Bitcoin mining hash rate, making it the second-largest mining country after the United States.

## Market Impact
