### EBITDA Surge: A Closer Look

The 33% surge in EBITDA is a significant achievement, driven by a combination of factors. One of the primary contributors to this growth is the strong performance of Umicore's Catalysis segment, which saw a 25% increase in revenue. This was largely driven by the growing demand for catalytic converters, particularly in the automotive sector. Additionally, the company's Energy & Surface Technologies segment also posted impressive results, with a 30% increase in revenue.

| Segment | H1 2025 Revenue | H1 2026 Revenue | YoY Growth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Catalysis | €1.23 billion | €1.54 billion | 25% |
| Energy & Surface Technologies | €0.85 billion | €1.11 billion | 30% |
| Recycling | €0.56 billion | €0.63 billion | 12% |

As shown in the table above, all three segments have contributed to the growth in revenue, with Catalysis and Energy & Surface Technologies being the primary drivers.

### Upgraded Guidance: What It Means for Investors

The upgraded guidance is a positive development for investors, as it indicates that Umicore is confident in its ability to maintain its strong performance throughout the remainder of 2026. The company has revised its full-year EBITDA guidance to €2.5-2.7 billion, up from the previous range of €2.2-2.4 billion. This upgrade is a testament to the company's ability to execute on its strategy and capitalize on growth opportunities.

#### Key Drivers Behind the Upgraded Guidance

Several factors have contributed to the upgraded guidance, including:

* Strong demand for catalytic converters, driven by the growing adoption of electric vehicles and the need for reduced emissions
* Increasing demand for energy storage solutions, particularly in the renewable energy sector
* The company's successful expansion into new markets, including Asia and the Americas
