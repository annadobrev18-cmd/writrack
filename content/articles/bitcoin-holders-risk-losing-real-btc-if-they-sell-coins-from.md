### Historical Context of Bitcoin Forks

Bitcoin forks are not a new phenomenon. The most notable fork in Bitcoin's history is the creation of Bitcoin Cash (BCH) in 2017, which resulted from a disagreement over the block size limit. Since then, numerous other forks have occurred, each with its own set of rules and potential outcomes for users. The BIP-110 fork, in particular, presents a unique set of challenges due to its specific implementation and the potential for confusion among users.

### Understanding BIP-110

BIP-110, or Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 110, refers to a specific set of changes proposed to the Bitcoin protocol. While details about the exact nature of BIP-110 are scarce in the context of this warning, the general principle behind any BIP is to enhance the functionality, security, or efficiency of the Bitcoin network. However, when a fork occurs, it can lead to the creation of a new blockchain that diverges from the original, potentially resulting in two versions of the truth regarding holdings.
