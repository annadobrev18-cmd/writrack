This move comes as the company begins to wind down its bitcoin mining activities, marking a strategic pivot in its business model. The partnership with AMD is expected to enhance Core Scientific's capabilities in AI computing, enabling the company to expand its service offerings beyond cryptocurrency mining.

### Historical Context
To understand the significance of this deal, it's essential to examine Core Scientific's history and the current state of the cryptocurrency market. However, the cryptocurrency market has experienced significant volatility in recent years, with the price of bitcoin fluctuating wildly.
