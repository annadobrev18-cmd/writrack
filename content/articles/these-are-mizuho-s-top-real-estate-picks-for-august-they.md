Mizuho's top pick in this sector is poised to benefit from this trend, with a strong portfolio of properties in prime locations.

However, Mizuho identifies a retail REIT that has successfully adapted to these shifts by focusing on experiential retail, grocery-anchored centers, and strategic locations with high foot traffic.

The global economy is interconnected, and events in one region can have far-reaching consequences. For real estate investors, understanding these global dynamics is crucial. The ongoing pandemic recovery, trade tensions, and geopolitical uncertainties can all influence property markets worldwide.

1. **Sustainability**: Investors are increasingly prioritizing environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors when making real estate investment decisions. Properties with high sustainability ratings can command premiums and attract a wider range of investors.
