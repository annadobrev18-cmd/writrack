## SunTrust's $240 Million Overdraft Fee Settlement: An Overview
SunTrust, a leading financial institution, has recently agreed to a $240 million settlement regarding overdraft fees. This development is significant, as it may result in eligible customers receiving up to $1,000 in compensation. The settlement is a culmination of a long-standing issue with the banking industry's practice of charging exorbitant overdraft fees.

### Background and Context

In recent years, several major banks have faced lawsuits and regulatory actions related to overdraft fees. This increased scrutiny has led to a shift in the banking industry, with many institutions re-evaluating their overdraft fee policies.

#### Historical Context
The issue of overdraft fees has been a contentious one for decades. In the early 2000s, banks began to aggressively market overdraft protection services, which allowed customers to overdraw their accounts in exchange for a fee. While these services were marketed as a convenience, critics argued that they were designed to generate revenue for banks at the expense of vulnerable customers.
