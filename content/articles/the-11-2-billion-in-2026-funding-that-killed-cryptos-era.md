## The Paradigm Shift in Crypto Funding
The cryptocurrency market has witnessed a significant transformation in recent years, with the $11.2 billion in funding in 2026 marking a pivotal point in its evolution. This substantial investment has led to a shift away from crypto's permissionless era, characterized by decentralized and unregulated transactions. The new funding landscape is expected to bring about increased regulatory oversight, potentially altering the fundamental nature of the crypto market.

### Historical Context: The Rise of Permissionless Crypto
The early days of cryptocurrency were marked by a lack of regulatory framework, allowing for the free flow of transactions without the need for intermediaries. This permissionless era was fueled by the decentralized nature of blockchain technology, which enabled peer-to-peer transactions without the need for central authorities. The rise of Bitcoin and other altcoins was a testament to the power of this model, with the total market capitalization of cryptocurrencies growing exponentially.
