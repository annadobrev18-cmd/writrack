#### Key Events
Some key events that have shaped the oil market in recent months include:
* The US withdrawal from the Iran nuclear deal, which led to a significant increase in tensions between the two countries
* The attack on Saudi Arabian oil facilities, which disrupted global supply and pushed prices higher
* The ongoing trade war between the US and China, which has impacted global demand for oil

## Impact of Trump's Decision on Oil Prices
The decision by President Trump to call off a military strike against Iran has had a significant impact on the oil market. The price of crude oil has declined sharply, with Brent crude falling to a three-week low. This move has been driven by a reduction in geopolitical risk, as the possibility of a conflict between the US and Iran appears to have decreased.

### Market Reaction
The market reaction to Trump's decision has been swift and decisive. Oil prices have fallen by over 5% in the past week, with the decline driven by a combination of factors, including:
* Reduced geopolitical risk
* Increased production from OPEC countries
* Weakening global demand

#### Technical Analysis
From a technical perspective, the decline in oil prices has been driven by a break below key support levels. The price of Brent crude has fallen below the $60 per barrel level, which had previously provided strong support. This move has opened up the possibility of further declines, with the next level of support located at $55 per barrel.
