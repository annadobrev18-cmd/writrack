## The Current State of the AI Bubble
The recent surge in AI-related stocks has led to concerns about a potential bubble in the market. As investors flock to companies involved in artificial intelligence, the valuations of these companies have skyrocketed, raising questions about their sustainability. According to a recent report, the AI market is expected to reach $190 billion by 2025, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 33.8%. However, this rapid growth has also led to concerns about a potential bubble, with some analysts warning that the market may be due for a correction.

### Historical Context: The Dot-Com Bubble
The current AI bubble bears some resemblance to the dot-com bubble of the late 1990s. During that time, investors poured money into internet-related stocks, driving their valuations to unsustainable levels. When the bubble burst, many of these companies went bankrupt, leaving investors with significant losses. Similarly, the AI bubble has led to inflated valuations, with some companies trading at price-to-earnings ratios of over 100. However, there are also some key differences between the two bubbles. For example, the AI bubble is driven by real technological advancements, rather than just speculation.

This diversity could help the index to remain resilient in the face of a market downturn. Additionally, the Japanese stock market has a reputation for being less volatile than other markets, which could make it a more attractive option for investors looking for a safe haven.
