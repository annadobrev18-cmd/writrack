### Understanding Form 144
Form 144 is a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) that discloses the intention of an insider to sell a significant amount of company stock. This filing is mandatory for insiders who plan to sell more than 5,000 shares or $50,000 worth of securities within a six-month period. The filing provides investors with valuable information about the company's insider activity, which can be a key indicator of the company's future prospects.
