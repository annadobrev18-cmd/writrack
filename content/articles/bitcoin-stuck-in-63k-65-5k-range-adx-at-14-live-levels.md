### Historical Context

To understand the significance of this range, it's essential to look at the historical context. Bitcoin's price has been on a bullish trend since the beginning of 2023, with the cryptocurrency reaching new all-time highs. However, the current range-bound pattern suggests that the market is consolidating and awaiting a catalyst for the next move.

#### Previous Breakout Attempts

In the past, Bitcoin has experienced similar range-bound patterns, followed by significant breakouts. For example, in 2020, Bitcoin's price was stuck in a range of $9K-$10K for several weeks before breaking out and reaching $64K. Similarly, in 2017, Bitcoin's price was range-bound between $1K-$1.2K before breaking out and reaching $20K.
