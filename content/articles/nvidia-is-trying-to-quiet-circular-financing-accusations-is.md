### Understanding Circular Financing

Circular financing refers to a practice where a company uses its own funds or resources to finance its operations, rather than relying on external sources of capital. This can create a false impression of financial stability and can be used to manipulate financial statements. In the case of Nvidia, accusations of circular financing have centered around the company's use of its own cash reserves to finance its operations, rather than relying on external investors.

### Historical Context
