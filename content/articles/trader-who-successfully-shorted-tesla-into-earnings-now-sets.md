### Market Impact: The Effect of Short Selling on Tesla's Stock Price
The bearish setup flagged ahead of Tesla's earnings report has had a significant impact on the company's stock price. Short selling, in particular, has played a crucial role in driving down the stock price. Short selling involves selling a security that the seller does not own, with the expectation of buying it back at a lower price to realize a profit.

#### The Mechanics of Short Selling
Short selling is a complex process that involves several steps. First, the trader borrows the security from a broker or another investor. The trader then sells the security at the current market price, with the expectation of buying it back at a lower price. If the stock price falls, the trader can buy back the security at the lower price and return it to the lender, realizing a profit.
