The 'Magnificent Seven' - consisting of Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, Facebook, Tesla, and NVIDIA - have been the darlings of the tech industry for years. Their innovative products and services, coupled with their dominant market positions, have made them a staple of many investment portfolios.
