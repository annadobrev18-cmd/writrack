## Fundamentals of Uber's Business
Uber Technologies, Inc. is a American technology company that operates a portfolio of businesses, including ride-hailing, food delivery, and freight transportation. The company's core business is its ride-hailing segment, which connects drivers with riders through its mobile app. Uber's business model is based on a commission-based structure, where the company takes a percentage of the fare paid by the rider.

### Revenue Streams
Uber generates revenue from various sources, including:
* Ride-hailing: This is the company's core business and generates the majority of its revenue.
* Food delivery: Uber Eats is the company's food delivery platform, which generates revenue through commission fees charged to restaurants and delivery fees charged to customers.
* Freight transportation: Uber Freight is the company's freight transportation platform, which connects shippers with carriers.
