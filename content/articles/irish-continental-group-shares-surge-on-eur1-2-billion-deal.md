### Background on Irish Continental Group

Irish Continental Group, a leading shipping, transport, and leisure company, operates extensively in the European market. Its subsidiary, Irish Ferries, is one of the primary operators of ferry services between Ireland, the UK, and France. The company's diverse portfolio also includes container shipping and logistics services through its Eucon and Feederlink divisions. With a strong presence in the European shipping market, Irish Continental Group plays a vital role in facilitating trade and travel between these regions.
