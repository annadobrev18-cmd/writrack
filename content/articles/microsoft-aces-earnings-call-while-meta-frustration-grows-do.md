## Meta's Earnings Call: A Growing Concern
In contrast, Meta's earnings call has been met with growing frustration from investors, who are increasingly concerned about the company's ability to deliver returns on its massive capital expenditures. Despite its significant investments in emerging technologies, Meta's financial performance has been underwhelming, with the company struggling to translate its innovations into tangible revenue growth.

### Meta's Financial Performance
The following table highlights Meta's key financial metrics:
| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 | YoY Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | $28.1B | $29.4B | -4.4% |
| Net Income | $6.2B | $7.5B | -17.3% |
| Operating Margin | 25.1% | 28.3% | -3.2% |
| Daily Active Users | 2.03B | 2.01B | 1.0% |

These numbers reveal Meta's struggles to drive growth, with revenue and net income declining year-over-year. The company's operating margin has also contracted, indicating increased pressure on its profitability.

### Competitor Analysis
A comparison of Microsoft and Meta's financial performance reveals significant differences in their strategic approaches and execution. While Microsoft has focused on developing and acquiring complementary technologies, Meta has invested heavily in emerging areas, such as virtual reality and e-commerce. However, Meta's inability to generate substantial returns on these investments has raised concerns among investors.

#### Historical Context
A review of the companies' historical financial performance reveals that Microsoft has consistently demonstrated a stronger ability to drive growth and profitability. The following table highlights the companies' revenue growth over the past five years:
