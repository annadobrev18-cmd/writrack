## Shift in Wealth Building Strategy
The recent surge in housing prices has led to a significant shift in the wealth building strategy of Gen Z and Millennials. With the housing market becoming increasingly unaffordable, these younger generations are turning to the stock market as a means of building wealth. According to a recent report, Gen Z and Millennials have reached a record high of $3.1 trillion in stock market holdings.

### Historical Context
To understand the magnitude of this shift, it's essential to look at the historical context of wealth building among younger generations. In the past, real estate was often considered a staple of wealth building, with many young people striving to own their own homes as a key milestone. However, with the rising costs of housing, this goal has become increasingly out of reach for many.
